Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AK Smarts ("we", "us") handles information when you visit aksmarts.com and its subdomains, including pdf.aksmarts.com (together, the "Sites"). We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000 of India.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: if you create an account or contact us, we collect your name, email address and anything you choose to write to us.
  • Usage information: basic, anonymous data such as pages visited, the kind of browser and device, and which tools are used. This helps us improve our apps.
  • Your files: our PDF and image tools process files inside your browser on your own device. We do not upload, read or store the contents of your files.

2. How we use information

  • To run, maintain and improve the Sites and apps.
  • To reply to your messages and give support.
  • To manage accounts, licences and e-signatures where you use those features.
  • To keep the Sites safe and prevent misuse.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Cookies and advertising

We use cookies and similar technologies to remember your settings and understand how the Sites are used.

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to our Sites and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some features may not work properly without them.

4. Sharing information

We share information only with service providers who help us run the Sites (for example hosting, email and advertising partners), when required by law, or to protect our rights and users' safety.

5. Data storage and security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. No method of sending or storing data online is completely secure, but we work hard to keep your data safe.

6. How long we keep data

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by law. You may ask us to delete your account and data at any time.

7. Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, or withdraw your consent, by writing to aksmartspdf@gmail.com. We will reply within a reasonable time.

8. Children

Our Sites are not meant for children under 18 without the consent of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Links to other sites

Our Sites may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

11. Contact and grievances

For any question or complaint about privacy, contact our Grievance Officer at aksmartspdf@gmail.com.
