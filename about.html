Our story

Why we started

AK Smarts started in India with a simple idea: everyday work software should be simple, affordable and respectful of your privacy. We began with PDF tools because documents are where most people lose time. More tools for daily office work are coming soon.

Our first product, AK Smarts PDF, brings 49+ PDF tools into one free app and keeps your files on your own device. More products coming soon.
