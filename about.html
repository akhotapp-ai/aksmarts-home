About AK Smarts

AK Smarts builds simple, fast and private web apps for everyday work.

Who we are

AK Smarts is an independent software studio based in India. We make tools that solve common, practical problems: the kind of small tasks that take far too long without the right app.

What we make

Our first product is AK Smarts PDF, a complete set of PDF tools that runs in the browser. You can compress, merge, split, convert, edit, sign (including with a Digital Signature Certificate) and protect PDF files on a phone or a computer. More apps are on the way, and each one will have its own address under aksmarts.com.

How we work

  • Privacy first. Wherever possible, your files are processed on your own device and never uploaded to our servers.
  • Simple to use. Clear screens and plain language, so anyone can get the job done.
  • Works everywhere. Our apps run in any modern browser on Windows, Mac, Android and iPhone.
  • Honest. No hidden charges and no surprise watermarks.
